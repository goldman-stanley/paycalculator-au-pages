Frequently asked questions

How much is $100,000 after tax in Australia?

With the defaults below, estimated monthly take-home pay is $6,452. That projects to $77,428 a year using 12 monthly pays. Figures are rounded to the nearest dollar.

Does a $100,000 salary include super?

This example treats $100,000 as salary excluding super. Employer super at 12% is $12,000 on top per year and is separate from take-home pay.

Does this estimate include HELP repayments?

HELP repayment is $0 because this example assumes no HELP debt. Enable HELP / HECS in the calculator to estimate its effect on your payslip.

Why might my payslip or tax return differ?

The estimate uses regular PAYG withholding for each pay frequency. Withholding includes income tax and Medicare; the calculator does not split these components. Annual take-home is the monthly estimate multiplied by 12. Pay-cycle rounding can cause small differences. Tax-return deductions, bonuses, purchased leave, salary sacrifice and personal circumstances can change the outcome.
