# PayCalculator AU Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 10 October 2026**

PayCalculator AU provides estimates for Australian salary, tax, Medicare levy, Medicare levy surcharge, superannuation and study loan repayments.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account and does not collect or transmit the salary, household income, insurance-day or study-loan amounts entered for calculations. Unsaved values are held in the current app session. If you tap Save, the app stores your named scenario and its calculation settings locally on your device. Language, colour theme and appearance preferences are also stored locally. The app does not operate a server or an account for this data. Device backups may include local app data according to your operating system settings. Sharing opens the system share sheet; you choose the destination and whether to send the generated result summary.

The app currently includes no advertising, analytics or third-party data collection SDKs. If you choose to open an Australian Taxation Office (ATO) link, the ATO website opens outside the app and handles any visit information under its own privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

You can load and delete locally saved scenarios in Saved scenarios. Reset clears the current calculation while keeping your saved scenarios and appearance preferences. Removing the app removes its local data; device backups are controlled by your operating system. There is no account or app-operated server copy. Any result you choose to share is handled by the receiving app or service under its own policy.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

## Contact

## General information

PayCalculator AU provides estimates for general information only. It is not tax or financial advice. Confirm tax rates, eligibility and individual circumstances with the ATO or a registered tax professional.
